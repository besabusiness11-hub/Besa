# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Small-to-medium local business owners in Italy (gyms, restaurants, professional studios) considering a custom website. Not technical, time-poor, price-sensitive, skeptical of agencies. They discover BESA via referral, Instagram/WhatsApp, or search, and land on either the main site or a niche funnel page (e.g. `/palestre` for gym owners). They're evaluating whether BESA is credible enough to trust with their business's online presence.

## Product Purpose

BESA is a web agency that designs and builds custom websites for local businesses in Italy. The main site is the agency's own portfolio/pitch; niche landing pages like `/palestre` are dedicated, vertical-specific funnels aimed at a single business type, built to convert a visitor into a Stripe checkout or a WhatsApp conversation with no sales call in between. Success is a visitor understanding what BESA does, trusting BESA enough to buy, and completing checkout or messaging without friction.

## Brand Personality

Veloce, Pratico, Funzionale — Fast, Practical, Functional. This is BESA's own stated slogan and should drive both copy and design: no fluff, clear payoff per section, nothing decorative that doesn't earn its place. Confident but not corporate; direct, informal "tu" register in Italian copy; a craftsperson's precision rather than a startup's hype.

## Anti-references

No specific named anti-references given. Generic constraint carried over from prior design work: avoid the default AI-landing-page look (cream+serif+terracotta, near-black+neon-accent, hairline-rule newspaper layout; tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows on every section; numbered 01/02/03 markers as decoration rather than genuine sequence; identical card grids; gradient text; side-stripe card borders).

## Design Principles

- Fast, practical, functional over decorative — every element should do a job.
- Show, don't tell — where possible, demonstrate the product (a real mockup, a real interaction) rather than describing it in copy.
- Ground each surface in its actual subject matter (a gym page should feel like a gym, not like generic SaaS) rather than reaching for category-default aesthetics.
- Trustworthy craftsmanship — the site selling web design must itself be impeccably designed; sloppy execution undermines the pitch.
- One deliberate signature moment per page, everything else calm and disciplined around it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard good practice, no formal WCAG compliance mandate: ≥4.5:1 body text contrast, ≥3:1 for large text, visible keyboard focus everywhere, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected with a real fallback (not just disabled, an alternative transition), semantic HTML and correct heading hierarchy.
