# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Italian owners of local businesses (any sector) who already pay for Meta (Facebook/Instagram) ads. Non-technical, time-poor, skeptical of agencies. Their ads point to an Instagram profile, a post, or a generic website, so clicks do not turn into leads. They arrive from referral, WhatsApp/Instagram or search, usually on a phone, and decide in seconds whether BESA is credible.

## Product Purpose

BESA builds landing pages made for Meta ad traffic: one goal, one action, fast. Offer hypothesis: monthly subscription (we build and keep updating on request), one-shot landing, or custom. This site is itself the demo of that offer. Success = a visitor submits the 3-field form (name, WhatsApp/email, link to their ad or profile).

## Brand Personality

Veloce, Pratico, Funzionale. Direct, informal "tu", a craftsperson's precision, no agency buzzwords. Two founders, Benito (product, tech) and Leo (business, strategy, marketing, content), speaking plainly.

## Anti-references

Generic agency template; cream+serif+terracotta; near-black+neon; magazine/editorial-typographic lane (display serif, hairline columns, mono labels); tiny uppercase eyebrows on every section; 01/02/03 markers as decoration; identical icon-card grids; gradient text; side-stripe borders; glassmorphism; hero-metric blocks; invented proof.

## Design Principles

- Show the problem, then the fix: the page demonstrates ad-to-profile vs ad-to-landing.
- One idea per fold, one action on the page.
- Fast is a feature: the speed of this site is part of the proof.
- Blue means action and emphasis, nothing else.
- One signature moment (hero ad-to-landing morph); everything else calm.
- No invented proof. Placeholders are marked [TODO].

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Body text >= 4.5:1, large text >= 3:1, visible focus on everything, 44px+ targets, semantic HTML with one H1, `prefers-reduced-motion` gets a real static/crossfade alternative.
